Research Project

AI that levels the financial playing field

Launched in 2024 at the intersection of machine learning, financial economics, and social justice, EquitAI is an open academic research project developing next-generation AI systems to price and predict asset values with near-perfect accuracy.

Our core hypothesis: When everyone — from retail investors in rural communities to institutional funds — has access to the same high-quality pricing signals, markets become fairer and systemic inequality shrinks.

Multimodal AI Models

Transformer networks + Graph Neural Networks trained on price data, news, satellite imagery, and on-chain transactions.

Equity-First Objective

Loss functions explicitly penalize models that increase wealth concentration. We optimize for both accuracy and fairness metrics.

Global Dataset

Covers 127 countries, 42 asset classes, and over 15 years of high-frequency data — including emerging markets often ignored by Wall Street.

Open & Reproducible

All code, weights, and datasets released under CC-BY-SA. Reproducible on consumer GPUs.

METHODOLOGY

How EquitAI predicts asset prices with radical accuracy

01

Multimodal Data Fusion

Prices, earnings transcripts, satellite imagery of factories, social sentiment, weather patterns, and geopolitical events are fused into a single latent representation.

02

Fairness-Constrained Training

We add adversarial fairness terms to the loss function so the model cannot exploit signals that disproportionately benefit high-net-worth individuals.

03

Causal Graph Neural Networks

Markets are modeled as evolving graphs. The model learns causal relationships between assets, not just correlations.
